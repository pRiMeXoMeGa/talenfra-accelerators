**How to read this:**

- "UNVERIFIED" means I could not confirm it from any source I could read.
- Some official pages would not load fully (404, sign-in walls, or the page text was empty). Where that happened I say so.

## 1. Y Combinator (YC)

### What it is
The best-known startup accelerator in the world. It invests money and runs a 3-month program in San Francisco ending in Demo Day. Since 2025 it runs **four batches a year** (Winter, Spring, Summer, Fall).

### Location / format
- In person in San Francisco. "The batch takes place in-person in San Francisco." Includes a 3-day retreat and weekly meetups. Founders can move elsewhere after the batch. OFFICIAL - https://www.ycombinator.com/faq (read 2026-10-01)

### Batch dates and deadlines
| Batch | Deadline | Decision | Program | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fall 2026 | Jul 27, 2026 (closed) | - | Oct-Dec 2026 | SECONDARY - https://www.roundfunded.com/en/blogs/yc-application-deadlines-2026-2027 |
| **Winter 2027** | **Nov 2, 2026 at 8pm PT** | **by Dec 11, 2026** | **January to March 2027, San Francisco** | OFFICIAL - https://www.ycombinator.com/apply |
| Spring 2027 | Not announced. Estimated around Feb 2027 | - | ~Apr-Jun 2027 | SECONDARY / estimate only |
| Summer 2027 | Not announced. Estimated around May 2027 | - | ~Jul-Sep 2027 | SECONDARY / estimate only |

- Late applications are still looked at after the deadline, but later. OFFICIAL - https://www.ycombinator.com/apply
- **Early Decision:** you can apply now for a future batch (Spring, Summer, Fall). OFFICIAL - https://www.ycombinator.com/apply. Useful if you want to get some customers first and join a later batch.
- YC only publishes the next deadline; later dates are guesses. SECONDARY - roundfunded (above).

### Money and equity
- "We invest $500,000": "$125,000 on a post-money SAFE in return for 7% of your company" plus "$375,000 on an uncapped SAFE with a Most Favored Nation ('MFN') provision". OFFICIAL - https://www.ycombinator.com/deal
- The MFN SAFE takes the best terms (lowest cap) of any SAFE you issue later before your priced round. OFFICIAL - same page.
- YC gets a pro-rata right (right to invest in later rounds). OFFICIAL - same page.
- "We don't charge any fees." OFFICIAL - same page.

### Perks
- Demo Day, the YC alumni network, partner office hours. Specific credit amounts: UNVERIFIED (not checked on an official page this time).

### Who they fund
- **Idea stage / pre-revenue:** "Approximately 40%" of funded companies are at idea stage with no revenue. OFFICIAL - https://www.ycombinator.com/faq
- **Solo founders:** "We regularly accept solo founders", but co-founders are encouraged. OFFICIAL - FAQ. (You have a co-founder, which helps.)
- **Not incorporated yet:** Incorporation is not needed before applying ("Nope"). OFFICIAL - FAQ.
- **Non-US / India founders:** Allowed. But the company must have a parent company in the **US, Canada, Singapore or Cayman Islands** to take part. So Talenfra would need a Delaware (or similar) parent. OFFICIAL - FAQ.
- **Reapplying:** About half of accepted companies applied more than once. OFFICIAL - FAQ.

### Visa support
- "YC works with excellent immigration attorneys that have helped hundreds of our founders get visas." OFFICIAL - https://www.ycombinator.com/faq

### Acceptance rate / batch size
- YC does not publish an acceptance rate on its FAQ. OFFICIAL - FAQ.
- Reported: around 1% (0.6%-2% depending on batch). Winter 2026 had 199 companies, Spring 2026 had 193. About 10,000+ applications per batch. SECONDARY - https://www.roundfunded.com/en/blogs/yc-batches-2026-dates-acceptance-rate and https://www.wearefounders.uk/y-combinator-acceptance-rate-2026/

### Application steps and what it asks
- Online form, about 20-30 questions on the startup, team and progress. SECONDARY - https://www.flowjam.com/blog/yc-application-tips-2025
- **Founder video: 1 minute.** All founders should speak, no script, no polish needed. SECONDARY - flowjam (above). YC says applicants who send videos are more likely to get interviews. OFFICIAL - https://www.ycombinator.com/howtoapply
- **Demo** (optional): YC looks at demos when given. OFFICIAL - howtoapply.
- Known questions: describe the company in 50 characters or less; what you are making; how far along you are (users, revenue); "something impressive that each founder has built or achieved" (OFFICIAL - howtoapply); the "hacked a non-computer system" question (OFFICIAL - howtoapply); legal entity / equity split among founders; competitors; why you; how you will make money. The full exact form text: **UNVERIFIED** (YC's application form needs a login; I could not read it).
- What they look for: clear, simple writing; strong founders; real insight into the problem. They warn against marketing-speak, hiding flaws, and saying "we will just execute better". OFFICIAL - https://www.ycombinator.com/howtoapply

### Interview process
- 10-minute Zoom with 2-3 YC partners. All founders must attend. They ask questions and look at what you built. Decision usually the same day, with feedback. OFFICIAL (from YC's interview guide page, as shown in search results) - https://www.ycombinator.com/interviews

### Rules on agencies / AI wrappers / HR-tech
- No written ban on HR-tech. YC has funded many recruiting companies (see alumni below).
- Fall 2026 Requests for Startups list (13 items) has **no recruiting/HR item**. OFFICIAL - https://www.ycombinator.com/rfs
- Commentators say YC prefers vertical AI agents with deep industry focus over "thin wrappers". SECONDARY - https://pinggy.io/blog/what_yc_is_funding_in_2026/ . No official "no wrappers" rule found: UNVERIFIED as a rule.

### Rejection reasons they publish
- YC does not give feedback to rejected (non-interviewed) applicants because of volume. OFFICIAL - FAQ.
- Common mistakes they name: unclear writing, marketing-speak, generic descriptions, hiding known problems, listing the startup itself as your achievement. OFFICIAL - howtoapply.

### Relevant alumni (OFFICIAL - https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/industry/recruiting-and-talent)
- **Spott (W2025)** - "first AI-native ATS/CRM for recruiting & staffing firms". Same customer as Talenfra. Important: a direct competitor-type company is already in YC.
- **Juicebox (S2022)** - AI recruiting/sourcing platform.
- **Alex (W2024)** - AI that interviews and screens candidates.
- Also: Perfectly (W2026, AI recruiting OS incl. screening), Candidately (S2017, AI workspace for recruitment agencies), Ashby (W2019), Dover (S2019).

### Fit score: **3 / 5**
Free to apply, accepts pre-revenue Indian founders, gives visa help, and clearly likes recruiting AI. But ~1% get in, the space already has YC companies (Spott, Perfectly, Alex), and with zero customers you have little "proof" to show; a few paying pilots before Nov 2 (or via Early Decision) would change the odds a lot.

## 2. Techstars

### What it is
A global accelerator network. Each program is 3 months of mentor-driven support, then Demo Day. Many programs are city-based or run with a corporate partner.

### Location / format
- Current programs listed: ABN AMRO Future of Finance, Northwestern Medicine Healthcare, AI Health Baltimore, Alabama EnergyTech, **Anywhere**, Boston, Boulder, Chicago, Columbus, Future of Food, Healthcare (Permanente), **London**, **New York City**, Space, Tokyo, USC. **No India program listed.** OFFICIAL - https://www.techstars.com/accelerators
- **NYC:** hybrid. First 2 weeks in person, one week in person mid-program, then Demo Day; virtual support in between. Founders "based anywhere globally". OFFICIAL - https://www.techstars.com/accelerators/new-york
- **London:** welcomes "early-stage founders from all over the world across all verticals". OFFICIAL - https://www.techstars.com/accelerators/london
- **Anywhere:** remote-first with 3 in-person offsites, but **"limited to companies in North American time zones"**, and focuses on robotics, energy, applied/physical AI, materials. Not a fit for an India-based team. OFFICIAL - https://www.techstars.com/accelerators/anywhere
- **Workforce Development accelerator** (the HR/future-of-work track): ran its 2026 cohort (deadline Nov 19, 2025; started Mar 9, 2026; virtual with optional in-person, Denver; 8 startups; focus incl. job matching). SECONDARY - https://opportunitydesk.org/2025/11/06/techstars-workforce-development-accelerator-2026/ . It is **not in the current list** of accelerators on techstars.com, so a 2027 cohort is UNVERIFIED.

### Batch dates and deadlines (Spring 2027 cycle)
- London, NYC, Anywhere: applications opened Aug 24, 2026; **final deadline Nov 18, 2026**; program starts **Mar 8, 2027**; **Demo Day Jun 3, 2027**. OFFICIAL - the three program pages above.
- Applications are rolling; after the deadline you go into a general pool. SECONDARY - startupresearcher (search result).
- Next cycle after that (Fall 2027): UNVERIFIED. Last year the Fall cycle deadlines were around June. SECONDARY - https://hub.causo.ai/guides/accelerator-application-deadlines-2026

### Money and equity
- $220,000 total: $20,000 via a Post-Money Convertible Common Equity Agreement for **5% common stock**, plus $200,000 via an **uncapped MFN SAFE**. OFFICIAL - https://www.techstars.com/accelerators/new-york
- Both convert at a priced round of at least $1M. SECONDARY - https://dabafinance.com/en/news/techstars-matches-y-combinator-with-updated-220k-investment-terms
- Fees: none stated. UNVERIFIED that there are zero fees.

### Perks
- "$2M+ in partner perks", mentors, investor network, lifetime support. OFFICIAL - NYC page.

### Who they fund
- Early-stage, any country; no need to be full-time before applying; full exclusivity if accepted; many arrive having raised $1M-$5M already (so the bar on traction is real). OFFICIAL - NYC page.
- Pre-revenue is possible, but they want some validation (LOIs, pilots, waitlist, users). SECONDARY - https://hub.causo.ai/guides/how-to-apply-to-techstars-2026 and Techstars Paris blog https://www.techstars.com/blog/advice/techstars-paris-the-ins-and-outs-of-the-application-and-selection-process
- Pre-incorporation / solo founders: UNVERIFIED.

### Visa support
- You can apply from any country, but you must be legally in the program country during the program. Techstars helps with immigration **only after acceptance**, through its network. OFFICIAL - https://help.techstars.com/support/solutions/articles/4000207088
- Legalpad (a Techstars partner) gives free visa office hours to Techstars founders. OFFICIAL (Techstars blog, older) - https://www.techstars.com/blog/startup-profile/legalpad-helping-immigrant-entrepreneurs-one-visa-at-a-time

### Acceptance rate / batch size
- Not published officially. Reported ~1-2%, around 10-12 companies per cohort. SECONDARY - https://hub.causo.ai/guides/accelerator-acceptance-rates-benchmark-2026 . Workforce 2026 had 8 companies (SECONDARY - above).

### Application steps and questions
- Apply at apply.techstars.com. One application can be sent to several programs. OFFICIAL (London page links there); multi-program detail SECONDARY.
- Part 1 founder profile (name, title, email, location, LinkedIn, full-time status); Part 2 ~18 fields on problem, solution, market/competition, team (bios, roles, how long you've worked together), traction (revenue, growth), next milestones; Part 3 pick programs. SECONDARY - https://hub.causo.ai/guides/how-to-apply-to-techstars-2026
- Video requirement: UNVERIFIED.

### Interview process
- Written review by Managing Director + team, then 1st round (~30 min with MD on team, customer discovery, KPIs), 2nd round on concerns, then a selection panel (mentors, partners, alumni). SECONDARY - causo guide above. Techstars Paris blog describes several rounds too (OFFICIAL blog, older).

### Rules on agencies / AI wrappers / HR-tech
- None found. NYC lists "future of work" and "AI/ML" as focus areas. OFFICIAL - NYC page.

### Rejection reasons they publish
- Team weighs most; weak traction/validation is a common issue. SECONDARY - causo guide. No official list found: UNVERIFIED.

### Relevant alumni
- Workforce 2026 cohort example: Essential Innovations (helps employers claim workforce tax credits). SECONDARY - opportunitydesk above. A strong AI-recruiting Techstars alumnus: UNVERIFIED (not found).

### Fit score: **2 / 5**
Good terms and London/NYC accept global founders, but the HR/workforce track is not open, Anywhere excludes Indian time zones, and visa help only starts after you're in. With no traction you'd compete against teams that already raised $1M+.

## 4. Antler (global + Antler India)

### What it is
A global early-stage VC that backs founders "from day zero", often before they have a team or idea. Present in ~27 cities. Runs "residencies" where founders build (and sometimes find co-founders), then Antler decides whether to invest. OFFICIAL - https://www.antler.co/faq (About text) and https://www.antler.co/apply

### Antler India - AI Residency (most relevant)
- **Where/format:** Bangalore, full-time and in person. **3 weeks** ("2 weeks of intense programming" + 1:1s with AI builders). Complimentary office space. OFFICIAL - https://www.antler.co/india
- **Money:** "INR 4Cr (~$470K) for 11% in total investment", "potential $30M in Follow-On Capital". **One-time INR 2 lakh grant** for expenses. OFFICIAL.
- **Perks:** "Over $1M in AI-Relevant Perks and upto $4M in total perks" (incl. ~$350k guaranteed Azure credits per SECONDARY - https://www.startupgrantsindia.com/antler-india-ai-residency-2nd-edition-352).
- **Decision speed:** "Funding decisions made in just 3 weeks." They "endeavour to invest in 50-75% of the teams that join" (another line says "60%+"). OFFICIAL.
- **Who:** "exceptional founding teams building promising AI solutions"; "Strong teams committed full-time; actively experimenting with users"; "pre-launch, pre-product, or pre-revenue stage"; "Available to join full-time in Bangalore". OFFICIAL. -> An existing two-person team with a built product and no revenue matches this wording.
- **Dates:** 2nd edition: deadline Feb 19, 2026, started Feb 23, 2026 (closed). SECONDARY - startupgrantsindia (above). **Next residency: "will be announced soon, till then keep reaching out to us directly."** OFFICIAL. No date as of 2026-10-01.
- **Tip from Antler itself:** "Getting a mutual friend to refer you to us works even better." OFFICIAL.

### Antler India - Embark (US market entry for Indian AI founders)
- Embark 3: Indian founders building AI-first startups; 4-6 weeks on-site in San Francisco (May 2026); applications closed Mar 28, 2026; decisions Apr 15, 2026; Antler commits **USD 450,000 per team** within pre-seed rounds of $600k-$800k; $1M+ credits; **help with visa, banking, compliance, company setup**; cohort of 4-5 teams; now open to non-portfolio founders. SECONDARY - https://india.entrepreneur.com/?p=94850 (article dated Mar 26, 2026). Embark 4 dates: UNVERIFIED.

### Antler global (other regions)
- **US (SF, NYC, Austin):** up to 3-month in-person residency; $500k-$1M initial commitment. **"Antler does not provide visa sponsorship."** At least one co-founder must already have unrestricted US work authorization. Solo founders welcome. OFFICIAL - https://www.antler.co/austin -> not usable for this applicant.
- **Singapore (2026 terms):** 6-week residency; $100k for 10% (SAFE) + $50k uncapped MFN SAFE + up to $250k matching = up to $400k. Wants teams with validated problem and early MVP. Visa help: not mentioned. OFFICIAL - https://www.antler.co/blog/antler-singapore-announces-improved-investment-terms-and-6-week-residency (post dated Jan 13, 2026).
- Nordics 2026: EUR 100k for 8.5% + EUR 100k uncapped SAFE. SECONDARY (search result citing antler.co blog).

### Application steps (global form)
- Name, email, LinkedIn (optional), funding stage ("Idea to Pre-Seed" or "Series A to C"), region/city; then optional company details, website, pitch deck (max 10MB). OFFICIAL - https://www.antler.co/apply
- India-specific questions / interview format: UNVERIFIED (locked behind sign-up per startupgrantsindia).

### Rules on agencies / AI / HR-tech; rejection reasons
- India residency is for "AI-native or AI-first" startups. SECONDARY - startupgrantsindia. No stated rules on HR-tech or services. No published rejection reasons. UNVERIFIED.

### Acceptance rate
- Not published for applications. Investment rate among residents: 50-75% (OFFICIAL, India page).

### Relevant alumni
- HireBound (AI recruitment) named as an Antler portfolio company in search results; details UNVERIFIED. Antler India has backed 115+ startups. SECONDARY - inc42 / yourstory search results.

### Fit score: **4 / 5** (Antler India AI Residency)
Bangalore (no visa needed), built for AI teams at pre-revenue stage, short (3 weeks), fast decision, and a strong local investor network. Weak point: no next date announced yet, and 11% is fairly expensive; Embark is the natural later path to the US.

## 5. Entrepreneur First (EF)

### What it is
A "talent investor". It backs **individuals** before they have a company, helps them find a co-founder and an idea, then invests. "We turn exceptional individuals into funded founders... then give you a bridge to Silicon Valley." Locations: London, Bangalore, San Francisco. OFFICIAL - https://www.joinef.com/

### Programs open now (OFFICIAL - https://apply.joinef.com/)
| Program | Where | Start |
|---|---|---|
| **Bangalore Program (+ SF extension)** | Bangalore then SF | **February 2027** |
| London Program (+ SF extension) | London | April 2027 |
| The Bridge Residency (young Europeans) | SF | February 2027 |
| XF2 (needs 7+ years experience and prior founding) | London | Fall 2026 |
| U.S. Funding & Fellowships | SF | rolling, 2026 |

- **Bangalore early deadline: "05 October 2026"** (first of three selection rounds). OFFICIAL - apply.joinef.com. Later round dates: UNVERIFIED.
- One secondary source says the Bangalore Winter 2027 cohort starts January 2027 and runs 14 weeks in Bangalore, then 3 months in SF. SECONDARY - startupresearcher (search result). The official apply page says February 2027 - use the official date.
- The Fall 2026 Bangalore cohort deadline was May 1, 2026 (closed). SECONDARY - https://www.startupgrantsindia.com/entrepreneurs-first-ef-bangalore-fall-26

### Money and equity (Bangalore)

- If the Investment Committee funds your team: **$125,000 post-money SAFE for 8%**, plus an optional extra **$125,000 uncapped MFN SAFE** from EF and Transpose Platform, which **requires moving to San Francisco and a Delaware company**. OFFICIAL - same page.
- Funded teams "relocate to San Francisco for LAUNCH, the final 12 weeks". OFFICIAL.
- General: "Equity-free grant during ideation phase", up to $250k at company formation, up to $5M follow-on through Series B. OFFICIAL - https://www.joinef.com/

### Perks
- $600K+ credits ($350K Azure, $250K+ from OpenAI, Anthropic, GitHub, PostHog, Datadog, ElevenLabs); nine months of office space. OFFICIAL - joinef.com. SF Demo Day to 200+ VCs. SECONDARY - startupgrantsindia.

### Who they fund
- Individuals; you can join with no idea and no co-founder. OFFICIAL - joinef.com.
- They want people with "continuous outperformance of peer groups", prior product/org/startup building experience, and ambition to build in the US. OFFICIAL - joinef.com.
- **Existing co-founders "may apply but should both submit applications."** OFFICIAL - apply.joinef.com. (The program is mainly designed for people without a co-founder - SECONDARY - causo guide.)
- Full-time, in person. OFFICIAL - apply.joinef.com.
- Existing company with a built product: no explicit rule found. UNVERIFIED.

### Visa support
- Yes: help "getting your visa" and relocation support (for the SF phase). OFFICIAL - joinef.com.

### Acceptance rate / batch size
- Not published. UNVERIFIED.

### Application steps and interview
- Apply at apply.joinef.com (individual application). Rolling review, then intro call with the Talent Investing team, then 1-3 interviews (technical + behavioural). SECONDARY - https://hub.causo.ai/guides/entrepreneur-first-application-examples-2026 and a founder blog https://dylancastillo.co/posts/my-entrepreneur-first-experience.html . Exact form fields: UNVERIFIED.

### Rules on agencies / AI / HR-tech; rejection reasons
- None published. UNVERIFIED.

### Relevant alumni
- **Omnipresent** (global employment / remote-work HR platform) - closest HR-related alumnus. Also Cleo, Tractable, Magic Pony (sold to Twitter). SECONDARY - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entrepreneurs_First . No AI-recruiting alumnus found.

### Fit score: **2 / 5**
Strong for network, visa help and a path to SF, and the Bangalore early deadline (Oct 5) is close. But EF is built for individuals still searching for a co-founder and idea; a team that already has both, plus a product, uses only the back half of what EF offers and must convince them each founder is an "outlier".

## Things that surprised me
1. **500 Global now charges a $35,000 program fee** (Phase 1) and only promises "up to $50K right away" - very different from the old $150k-for-6% deal. Its deadline is **Oct 2, 2026** (tomorrow).
2. **Techstars Anywhere is limited to North American time zones**, and the Techstars Workforce (HR/future-of-work) program is not in the current list.
3. **YC already has a company (Spott, W2025) selling an AI-native ATS/CRM to recruiting and staffing firms**, plus several AI screening companies (Alex, Perfectly). Good proof YC likes the space; harder to stand out.
4. **Antler US gives no visa sponsorship at all**, but **Antler India's Embark** program helps Indian AI founders move to the US, including visas.
5. **EF Bangalore pays INR 3.6 lakh on joining** and lets existing co-founders apply (both must apply separately).
