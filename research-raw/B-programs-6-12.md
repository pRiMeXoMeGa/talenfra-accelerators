## 1. South Park Commons (SPC) Founder Fellowship

**What it is.** A pre-seed fellowship and community for technical founders, from "no idea yet" to early company. Small cohorts with one partner per two companies.

**Location / format.** In person. Bootcamp starts with a retreat in Northern California, then SF programming. Fellows must work from an SPC office in **San Francisco, New York or Bangalore** during the bootcamp; they can move afterwards. After the bootcamp there is a "residency" phase until the seed round. Optional "Demo Faire".
- Source: https://www.southparkcommons.com/news/f26-founder-fellowship (official, read 2026-10-01)

**Dates and deadlines.**
- Fall 2026: deadline was **Aug 2, 2026, 11:59pm PT**. Interview invites by Aug 30. Bootcamp late Sep to late Nov 2026. **Closed.**
- Spring 2026 (for the pattern): deadline Feb 1, 2026; interview invites by Feb 28; bootcamp late March to late May. Source: https://blog.southparkcommons.com/spc-founder-fellowship-spring-2026/ (official blog)
- **Spring 2027 deadline: not announced. UNVERIFIED.** Based on the pattern, expect early Feb 2027, with applications opening around Dec/Jan.

**Money and equity.** "$400K upfront for 7% on a standard SAFE" plus "$600K guaranteed in your next external funding round". Up to $1M in credits and perks (Anthropic, OpenAI, Microsoft, GCP, AWS and others). Source: f26 page above.

**Who they fund.**
- Stage: idea stage is fine. "No idea or traction required."
- Solo: yes, but they prefer people who can build and prototype.
- Sector: sector-agnostic, but wants "ambition to build at the frontier".
- Existing company with a product: yes, can apply; they will look at its progress. Source: https://www.southparkcommons.com/faq (official)
- Non-US / India: yes. Bangalore is one of three hubs.
- Pre-incorporation: no stated rule. UNVERIFIED.

**Visa.** SPC "cannot directly sponsor visas" but is "happy to discuss your circumstances". Source: FAQ above. For this founder, the **Bangalore hub removes the visa problem**.

**Batch size / acceptance rate.** About 25 companies per cohort (3rd-party: https://startupintros.com/news/2025-03-24-spc-founder-fellowship-spring-2025 and other startupintros pages). Acceptance rate: not published. UNVERIFIED.

**How to apply.** Online form at southparkcommons.com/apply. The official page only says the form should show "how you generate ideas" (technical approach, research, markets, ideas you have considered). Exact questions: **UNVERIFIED** (form not readable without starting it). Then: reply in 1-3 weeks, then interview(s). Exact number of rounds: UNVERIFIED.

**Services / HR-tech rules.** None stated.

**Fit: 3/5.** Bangalore hub + strong name + big cheque is great for network and credibility. But SPC leans toward "frontier" tech and strong technical builders; an applied AI tool for recruiters with no users is a weaker story here than at an enterprise-focused program.

## 2. Alchemist Accelerator (flagship)

**What it is.** A 6-month accelerator only for enterprise-focused startups: "AI-SaaS and deeptech companies that monetize from enterprises". B2B and B2B2C welcome.
- Source: https://www.alchemistaccelerator.com/ and https://www.alchemistaccelerator.com/flagship-program (official)

**Location / format.** Hybrid. Weekly sessions (usually Thursdays). Required in-person kick-off week in the SF Bay Area. Virtual Demo Day, plus an optional in-person preview a month earlier. Free co-working in SF. Other tracks: Alchemist Chicago, Doha, Japan.

**Dates and deadlines.** The official site still says the next class "begins on January 22, 2026" (stale as of today). Decisions come within 3 weeks of the deadline you apply under. **Next class date and deadline: UNVERIFIED.** A 3rd-party guide also says Alchemist does not publish a fixed calendar (https://hub.causo.ai/guides/how-to-apply-to-alchemist-accelerator-2026). Action: check the apply page or email admissions@alchemistaccelerator.com.

**Money and equity.** "Single-digit equity grant (typically 5%)". Average net investment about **$30,000 after the tuition offset** (there is a tuition fee, paid out of the investment). Alchemist may ask for minority co-investment rights in later rounds. Partner perks worth $450K+ (average used $175K). Source: official pages above.

**Who they fund.**
- Stage: no revenue minimum stated. In practice they value enterprise traction (pilots, LOIs, named buyers) — 3rd-party guide above.
- Team: 2-3 co-founders preferred; solo at a higher bar. (This applicant is a duo = fine.)
- International: yes, "Alchemist companies may be located anywhere in the world"; flexible on country of incorporation.
- Pre-incorporation: not stated. UNVERIFIED.

**Visa.** Not mentioned. UNVERIFIED (assume none).

**Batch size / acceptance.** About 25 teams and ~3% acceptance, from 3rd-party sources only (https://elev-x.com/news-insights/article-alchemist-accelerator-review/, dated 2026-06-23). Not official.

**How to apply.** 1) Online form. 2) Answer within ~3 weeks. 3) **15-minute virtual panel interview**. 4) Offer the following week. Exact form fields: **UNVERIFIED**.

**Services / HR-tech rules.** HR-tech not named, not excluded. Services companies not addressed.

**Fit: 3/5.** The best "shape" match on this list: B2B enterprise AI, international-friendly, mostly remote, 6 months fits slow B2B sales. But they want enterprise buyer evidence, and Talenfra has zero customers. Apply after 2-3 pilots for a much better chance.

## 3. Plug and Play (PnP)

**What it is.** A huge corporate-innovation platform (60+ locations, 25+ industry verticals). It matches startups to big corporate partners for pilots. It is not a classic cash-for-equity accelerator.

**Official site note.** plugandplaytechcenter.com pages (/startups, /faq) render no text for my tools. Facts below are mostly 3rd-party. Treat as partly UNVERIFIED.

**Money and equity.** Program participation: **no equity, no fees** (3rd-party: https://hub.causo.ai/guides/how-to-apply-to-plug-and-play-2026). An official PnP page confirms at least some programs are "equity-free" (CASSINI program: https://welcome.plugandplaytechcenter.com/success-story-of-cassini-business-accelerator). PnP Ventures (separate) may invest, reported $50K-$250K — 3rd-party, UNVERIFIED.

**Dates.** Per vertical and location. Silicon Valley first 2026 batches took 113 startups (3rd-party, causo guide). No HR/Future-of-Work PnP program found for 2026.

**India.** Only a GIFT City fintech program with PnP (Indian-registered fintech startups; window Jun 10 to Jul 5, 2026): https://www.startupgrantsindia.com/gift-ifih-residential-program-batch-2 (3rd-party). Not relevant to Talenfra.

**Who they fund.** Usually post-MVP with some traction, and a product that solves a named corporate problem. Pre-revenue is not banned but corporates want proof.

**Visa.** None found. UNVERIFIED.

**How to apply.** Pick a vertical and a location, then apply on that program's page; selection starts with a startup meeting; good fits may be invited to PnP's "Playbook" platform (3rd-party).

**Fit: 2/5.** Free and good for corporate intros, but the buyers PnP brings are big corporates, not recruiting/staffing agencies, and there is no India or HR-tech track found. Low credibility value at this stage.

## 4. Peak XV Surge (formerly Sequoia India & SEA Surge)

**What it is.** Peak XV's seed program for India, Southeast Asia and global founders. Seed money plus "full-stack support" (hiring, product, GTM, PR, policy, global expansion).
- Source: https://surge.peakxv.com/ (official)

**Money.** Official: **$500K to $5M** seed capital, open to co-investors. Equity %: not published. UNVERIFIED. News: Surge 11 (announced Sep 26, 2025) gave **up to $3M** per startup, 23 startups (12 AI-first): https://india.entrepreneur.com/news-and-trends/peak-xvs-surge-11-features-23-startups-across-ai-fintech/497590

**Dates.** The apply form is **rolling** and says you hear back in **2-3 weeks**: https://surge.peakxv.com/apply. I found no news of a 2026 cohort (Surge 12). UNVERIFIED whether a 2026 cohort ran or is planned.

**Who they fund.** Stage choices on the form go from idea/prototype to launched. The form asks for the **country where the company is registered**, so incorporation is expected. India: yes (offices in India, Singapore, US, UAE). Solo/duo: form has co-founder fields; no rule stated.

**Visa.** Not relevant (India-based).

**Application items (official form, 4 sections):**
- Company: name, website, country of registration, category (29 options incl. AI/ML), where customers/users are.
- Founders: full name, phone, email, LinkedIn; each co-founder's name, email, phone, LinkedIn, city; founder background/story.
- Narrative: one-sentence description; the problem; what makes you different; why your team; stage; traction (or "N/A").
- Optional: deck, demo, screenshots, Loom video (via link); references (name, email, relationship); have you talked to anyone at Surge before.
- Interview process: not published. UNVERIFIED.

**Batch size.** 13 (Surge 9), 14 (Surge 10), 23 (Surge 11) per news above. Acceptance rate: not published.

**Fit: 2/5.** Big credibility in India, but Surge backs teams that can raise a $1-3M seed; a pre-customer, non-incorporated team with no notable background is a long shot. Worth one cheap application once incorporated, with a Loom demo.

## 5. a16z speedrun

**What it is.** a16z's accelerator, started 2023, sector-agnostic, strong AI focus. 300+ startups backed, $300M+ invested.
- Sources: https://speedrun.a16z.com/, https://speedrun.a16z.com/faq, https://speedrun.a16z.com/apply (official)

**Location / format.** "IRL in San Francisco"; **in-person attendance is required**. ~11-12 weeks.

**Dates.**
- SR007: deadline May 17, 2026; ran Jul 27 to Oct 11, 2026; Demo Day Oct 6. Source: https://a16z.com/applications-for-a16z-speedrun-sr007-are-now-open/
- **SR008: starts early 2027 (late Jan to April 2027). Priority window Oct 12 to Nov 1, 2026** ("fastest review"). Applications accepted year-round. Review usually 4-6 weeks.

**Money.** Up to $1M: **$500K for 10% upfront via SAFE**, plus **$500K in your next round within 18 months**. Pro-rata request. No board seat. "$10M+" in partner credits (official site; the SR007 post says $5M+).

**Who they fund.**
- Any background; no prior funding needed; must be **full-time**.
- Solo: "absolutely" can apply; teams preferred.
- International: welcome. India listed among founder countries.
- Pre-incorporation: allowed in effect — a16z helps "set up your pool as well as company incorporation as needed".

**Visa.** Yes: the **Global Founders Program** helps with visa petitions, consulate interviews and border crossing. You can apply before having a visa.

**Acceptance.** Official FAQ: "lower than 0.4%"; recent cohorts 60-70 teams. SR006 had 19,000+ applicants (3rd-party: https://startupintros.com/news/2026-04-14-a16z-speedrun-sr006).

**How to apply.** Enter email at speedrun.a16z.com/apply and fill the form (exact questions **UNVERIFIED** — hidden behind sign-up). Then: invitation to a short video pitch or a **15-minute interview**; they may ask for references, data, details. Contact: sr-team@a16z.com.

**Services / HR-tech rules.** None stated.

**Fit: 3/5.** Free to apply, has visa help, takes pre-incorporation teams, and the Oct 12 to Nov 1 window is right now. But 0.4% acceptance; with no users the odds are tiny. Apply anyway (low cost), with a strong demo video and any early pilot signal.

## Ranked opinion: which belong in the "best 10" (with YC, Techstars, 500 Global, Antler, EF)

My pick of 5 from this set, in order:

1. **a16z speedrun** — apply now in the Oct 12 to Nov 1, 2026 window. Takes pre-incorporation teams, has real visa help, huge credibility. Very low odds, but cheap to try.
2. **South Park Commons Founder Fellowship** — Bangalore hub means no visa issue; $400K for 7%; strong network. Target Spring 2027 (watch for ~Feb 2027 deadline).
3. **Alchemist Accelerator** — the best fit by shape (enterprise B2B AI, international, hybrid, 6 months). Apply once there are 2-3 pilots or LOIs.
4. **Nasscom GenAI Foundry** — exact HR/talent track and India enterprise network, no equity. Watch for the next cohort.
5. **Google for Startups Accelerator: India** — equity-free, high credibility in India; needs some traction; target ~Apr 2027 deadline.

Honourable mentions (not "accelerators" in the same sense, but worth doing): **SHRM Labs WorkplaceTech** (HR-tech gold standard, needs paying customers — aim for the 2027-28 cycle), **NVIDIA Inception + Microsoft for Startups** (free perks right after incorporation), **Peak XV Surge** (one application after incorporating).

Leave out: Plug and Play (wrong buyers, no India/HR track), Axilor (now a seed fund, not an accelerator), Neo (likely North America only), Founders Inc (hardware), Pioneer (closed).

**Big common blocker:** almost every program here (Surge, NVIDIA, Microsoft, SHRM, SISFS, Google India) wants an **incorporated company**, and the better ones want **some customer proof**. Incorporating (Pvt Ltd or a US C-corp if going the US route) and landing 2-3 pilot recruiting firms would move most fit scores up by 1-2 points.
