What to finish first

Must do decides the application

  1. Talk to 15–30 staffing firms; get 2–5 to run a real role.
  2. Put the engine live at app.talenfra.com.
  3. Founder agreement, vesting, IP assignment; decide with lawyers and a CA whether to set up a Pvt Ltd now (optional) or flip after acceptance.
  4. Remove website promises the engine cannot keep (ATS, Sheets, Slack, video, accuracy numbers).
  5. Write one sharp answer to "why not Bullhorn or LinkedIn?"
  6. Settle founder facts: full-time, who codes, third person's role.

Should do raises the odds

  1. 1-minute founder video, demo, 10-slide investor deck, metrics sheet.
  2. Choose a pricing story that scales.
  3. Finish the DPA and write a 1-page security and bias note.
  4. Fix the erasure gaps in the engine before real CVs.
  5. Measure agreement with a recruiter's own shortlist.
  6. Check passports and visa appointment waits.

Three things to know before you start

Apply first, flip later

YC needs no company to apply, but its money goes to a US, Canada, Singapore or Cayman company. Indian founders need a CA and a FEMA lawyer for the flip (an LLP per founder is the usual route).

Visas

E-2 is closed to Indians. Most YC founders come on B-1, then O-1A. H-1B now costs $100,000. Bangalore programs need no visa.

AI hiring laws

EU AI Act high-risk rules start 2 Dec 2027; Colorado 1 Jan 2027; NYC, Illinois and California already apply. Your evidence-for-every-score design helps here.

Scams

Real accelerators pay you. Walk away from application fees, fee plus equity, and unnamed mentors. 500 Global is real but now charges $35,000.
