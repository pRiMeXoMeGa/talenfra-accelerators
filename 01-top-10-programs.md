## How the ranking works
The order is **not** the fit score alone. It blends three things: how good the program is for credibility and network (YC, a16z first), how well it fits you today (fit score), and whether you can act on it now (an open deadline). So YC (fit 3) is #1 on prestige and a live deadline, Antler India (fit 4) is #3 because it has no open date, and Entrepreneur First (fit 2) sits above Nasscom (fit 4 only if a cohort opens) because EF has a confirmed date and a visa route while Nasscom's 2026 cohort is unconfirmed. Treat the fit column as the honest match score and the order as "what to look at first".

## What I would call "best for you" (a recommendation — you decide)

- **Best fit for your situation today:** Antler India AI Residency. Bangalore (no visa), pre-revenue is welcome, 3 weeks, answer in 3 weeks, they try to invest in 50–75% of the teams that join. Weak point: no date yet, and 11% is a high price.
- **Best for credibility and network:** Y Combinator. Free to apply, accepts pre-revenue, no company needed to apply, and funds many AI-recruiting companies. But about 1% get in.
- **Best "shape" match (B2B enterprise AI):** Alchemist, once you have 2–3 pilots.
- **Best India HR-tech match:** Nasscom GenAI Foundry — the only program found with an HR/talent track — but a 2026 cohort is not confirmed.

## Three notes on the numbers

1. **500 Global changed its deal.** Many guides still say "$150K for 6%". The official page (read 1 Oct 2026) says fee $35,000 and "up to $50K". Trust the official page.
2. **Equity maths:** in one illustrative example (YC at 7% plus an MFN SAFE, a $1.5M SAFE round at a $15M cap, then a priced round with a 10% option pool), two founders end near 28% each. Real numbers will differ (worked examples in `07-other-findings.md`). The next round's cap matters more than the accelerator's percentage.
3. **Deadlines move.** Re-check each official page before you apply. Every date here was read on 2026-10-01.
