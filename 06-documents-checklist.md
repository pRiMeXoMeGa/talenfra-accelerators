## E. Practical checklist before pressing "submit"
- ☐ Read the live form on the official page (dates and questions change).
- ☐ Both founders read the full application.
- ☐ Every claim can be shown in the product or a document.
- ☐ Website no longer promises what the engine cannot do.
- ☐ Demo link works from a phone and a fresh browser.
- ☐ You never paid an application or "pitch" fee (see scam red flags in `07-other-findings.md`).
