# 07 — Other important things you should know

Full details with links: `research-raw/D-legal-india-visa-other.md`. I am not a lawyer or CA. Items marked **[LAWYER/CA]** must be confirmed by a professional. Facts read 2026-10-01.

## 1. You can apply before you have a company — but you need one to take the money
- **YC:** "Do we need to be incorporated to apply? Nope." But to invest, the company must be incorporated in the **US, Canada, Singapore or Cayman Islands**.
- **Techstars:** India is listed as a country that must reorganise before investment.
- **EF:** takes individuals; Indian companies can get preference shares (CCPS). The extra $125K needs a Delaware company and a move to SF.
- **Antler India:** invests with an INR cheque; exact entity rules are UNVERIFIED (not on its page).
- **Advice from the research:** apply first, flip after acceptance. A flip costs money every year (Delaware tax, US + India filings, transfer pricing).

## 2. The India catch (important)
- An Indian resident generally **cannot personally control a foreign company that owns an Indian company** (Overseas Direct Investment rules under FEMA). The route in Stripe Atlas's official India guide: each founder forms an **Indian LLP**, the LLPs hold the US shares, and the US company later owns the Indian Pvt Ltd. Needs an authorised-dealer bank, a UIN, Form FC, then yearly filings (APR by 31 Dec, FLA by 15 Jul). **[CA + FEMA lawyer essential.]**
- Keep the structure flat; the rules limit layers of subsidiaries. **[LAWYER/CA]**
- Moving the sole proprietorship's code, brand and contracts into a company is a transfer of an asset — tax and valuation questions. **[CA]**
- Flipping out is cheap now; flipping back to India later can be very expensive (big Indian startups paid thousands of crores in 2024–26). Decide with a lawyer.
- A sole proprietorship **cannot take equity investment** and is **not eligible for DPIIT startup recognition**. A Pvt Ltd or LLP would allow both, but DPIIT excludes businesses "formed by splitting or reconstructing an existing business", so **[CA]** must confirm whether converting your proprietorship counts.
- Angel tax was abolished from 1 Apr 2025. The Income-tax Act 2025 replaced the 1961 Act from 1 Apr 2026, so old articles quote old section numbers.

**SAFE in plain words:** the investor gives cash now and gets shares later, at your next priced round. A *post-money* SAFE locks the investor's percentage at signing; every later SAFE dilutes only the founders. An *uncapped MFN* SAFE copies the best terms of your next SAFE.
**Example (illustrative):** with YC, a later $1.5M SAFE round at a $15M cap, then a priced round selling 20% and a 10% option pool, two founders end near **28% each** (about 56% together). The next round's cap matters more than the accelerator's percentage.

## 4. Visas
- **E-2 is not open to Indians** (no treaty).
- Most international YC founders attend on a **B-1 business visitor visa** and move to **O-1A** later (per a secondary source, tryalma; immigration attorneys say the same). YC introduces you to immigration lawyers but does not issue visas. **[Immigration lawyer to confirm what you may do on B-1.]**
- **H-1B** now carries a **$100,000** fee for new petitions from abroad (extended on 18 Sep 2026 to 21 Sep 2027; court challenge ongoing). Not practical for you.
- Most US visa **interview waivers ended 2 Sep 2025** — expect long waits in India. Start on day one after acceptance.
- Alternatives: **UK Innovator Founder** visa (live), **Singapore EntrePass** (live; YC backing counts), **Canada Start-up Visa is closed**. Estonia, Netherlands, France details unverified.
- Programs in **Bangalore** (Antler India, EF first phase, SPC hub) need no visa.

## 5. What investors will ask about your product's legal risk
CV screening is "high-risk" in several laws:
- **EU AI Act:** recruitment tools are high-risk; obligations now apply from **2 Dec 2027** (delayed in 2026).
- **US:** NYC Local Law 144 (annual independent bias audit, candidate notice) with stricter enforcement in 2026; Illinois HB 3773 (since 1 Jan 2026); California employment AI rules (since 1 Oct 2025) and privacy ADMT rules (from 1 Jan 2027); Colorado SB 189 (from 1 Jan 2027).
- **Privacy:** GDPR/UK GDPR needs standard contract clauses for data going to India; India's DPDP Rules start core duties around May 2027. **[Privacy lawyer to confirm which SCC module applies, and how DPDP applies to foreign candidates' data processed in India.]**
- **[Lawyer to confirm]** whether Talenfra is the "provider" under the EU AI Act, and how each US state law applies to a vendor versus the recruiter client.
- **Court case:** *Mobley v. Workday* is a nationwide age-bias case against an AI screening vendor — vendors can be sued.
- **Expect to show:** a DPA, privacy policy, security overview, a short bias/oversight note, a clean IP chain, and a SOC 2 roadmap.
- **Good news:** evidence for every score and human approval are exactly what these laws want.

## 6. Founder agreement and IP — do this early
- Decide the split (about 46% of two-founder teams split equally; fair, written and full-time matters most).
- Vesting norm: 4 years with a 1-year cliff, including founders.
- Everyone who wrote code or designs must assign the IP to the company. Today the code is under one person's proprietorship. **[Lawyer to draft.]**
- 83(b) election must be filed within 30 days of getting US shares, with no extensions. **[US tax adviser.]**

## 7. Scam and quality warnings
Red flags: an application or "pitch" fee; a fee **and** equity; unnamed mentors; alumni you cannot verify; pressure to pay fast; "guaranteed funding". Rule: **real accelerators pay you.** Check alumni outcomes on Crunchbase/LinkedIn and talk to three recent founders. (500 Global is a real firm but now charges $35,000 — weigh that carefully.)
